# Pour Over

Pour over is a manual brewing method that gives baristas more control over extraction. It creates a cup with a clean finish and a more nuanced flavor profile.

## Ingredients

- 15–18 g coffee grounds
- 240–300 ml hot water
- Filter paper

## Method

1. Rinse the filter paper with hot water.
2. Add the ground coffee to the dripper.
3. Bloom with 30–40 g of water for 20–30 seconds.
4. Pour the remaining water slowly in circular motions.
5. Let the coffee drain completely.
6. Serve immediately for the best aroma and clarity.

## Flavor Notes

- Clean and bright
- More fruit, floral, or citrus notes than espresso
- Light to medium body

## Best For

- Customers who enjoy clarity and nuance
- Single-origin beans
- Slow, mindful coffee moments
