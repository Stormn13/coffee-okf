# Barista Shift Guide

Welcome to your shift. Please review the following resources:

## Core Responsibilities
1. **Memorize the menu:** Review our [Drink Menu](../menu.md) for standard pricing.
2. **Handle complaints:** Always follow the [Refund Policy](../policies/refund-policy.md).
3. **Hardware checks:** Ensure the [Fridge Sensor](../hardware/fridge-sensor.md) is active and the OLED screen is on before opening.

## Recepies
1. **Espresso:** Check the [recepie](../recepies/espresso.md) to know how to prepare it.
2. **Latte:** Check the [recepie](../recepies/latte.md) to know how to prepare it.
3. **pour-over:** Check the [recepie](../recepies/pour-over.md) to know how to prepare it.
