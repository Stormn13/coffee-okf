# Espresso

Espresso is the foundation for many café drinks. It is brewed by pushing hot water through a compact puck of finely ground coffee under pressure.

## Ingredients

- 18 g espresso beans
- Water at approximately 93°C / 200°F

## Method

1. Grind coffee to a fine, powder-like consistency.
2. Dose 18 g into the portafilter.
3. Tamp evenly to create a level puck.
4. Lock the portafilter in the espresso machine.
5. Extract for about 25–30 seconds.
6. Aim for a rich, syrupy shot with a caramel-colored crema.

## Flavor Notes

- Bold and concentrated
- Slightly sweet and syrupy
- Can have notes of chocolate, caramel, or citrus depending on the beans

## Best For

- Standalone espresso shots
- Lattes, cappuccinos, and Americanos
- Customers who prefer a strong, compact coffee
