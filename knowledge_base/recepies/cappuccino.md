# Cappuccino

A cappuccino is known for its balanced texture: espresso, steamed milk, and thick foam in equal parts or close to it.

## Ingredients

- 1 double shot espresso
- 120–150 ml steamed milk
- 120–150 ml milk foam

## Method

1. Pull a double espresso.
2. Steam milk until warm and velvety.
3. Pour milk and foam into the espresso.
4. Finish with a light dusting of cinnamon or cocoa if requested.

## Flavor Notes

- Rich and full-bodied
- Stronger coffee taste than a latte
- Thick, velvety foam texture

## Best For

- Customers who prefer a richer foam profile
- Traditional espresso lovers
- People who enjoy a fuller milk texture with less sweetness
