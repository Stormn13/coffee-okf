# Americano

An Americano is a simple espresso drink made by adding hot water to one or more shots of espresso, creating a familiar drip-style strength.

## Ingredients

- 1–2 shots espresso
- 120–240 ml hot water

## Method

1. Pull the espresso shots.
2. Add hot water to a mug or cup.
3. Pour in espresso slowly.
4. Adjust the amount of water for strength preference.

## Flavor Notes

- Smooth and clean
- Less concentrated than straight espresso
- Works well for customers who like coffee but not bitterness

## Best For

- Customers wanting a longer drink without milk
- Morning coffee with a classic profile
- Espresso lovers wanting a lighter cup
