# Latte

A latte balances espresso with steamed milk and a small amount of foam. It is usually gentler and creamier than a cappuccino.

## Ingredients

- 1 double shot espresso
- 180–240 ml steamed milk
- Small layer of milk foam

## Method

1. Pull a double espresso shot.
2. Steam milk until it is silky and warm.
3. Pour the milk slowly into the espresso.
4. Add a thin layer of foam on top.
5. Optionally finish with cinnamon or vanilla syrup.

## Flavor Notes

- Smooth and mellow
- Slightly sweet due to milk
- Balanced between coffee strength and texture

## Best For

- Customers who want a softer coffee flavor
- Morning drinks and casual café favorites
- Anyone who enjoys milk-forward espresso beverages
