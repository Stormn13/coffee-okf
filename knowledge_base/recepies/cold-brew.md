# Cold Brew

Cold brew is made by steeping coarse coffee grounds in cold water for an extended period. The result is a less acidic, smooth coffee with a naturally sweet finish.

## Ingredients

- 20 g coarse coffee grounds
- 240 ml cold water
- Ice for serving

## Method

1. Combine grounds and cold water in a steeping container.
2. Let it steep for 12–18 hours in the refrigerator or a cool area.
3. Filter the liquid to remove grounds.
4. Serve over ice.
5. Add syrup or milk if desired.

## Flavor Notes

- Smooth and sweet
- Lower acidity than hot coffee
- Great for a refreshing finish

## Best For

- Warm weather service
- Customers who prefer sweeter, less acidic coffee
- Iced coffee drinkers
